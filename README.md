# Project Description

## Candidate Project 1
### Research Question
We want to investigate the use of an AI model to predict sleep apnea in adults using a combination of physiological measures from the [Respiratory and pulse oximetry waveforms form healthy adults during simulated apnoea events](https://physionet.org/content/respiratory-oximetry-apnoea/1.0.0/) dataset. 

### Biggest Uncertainty 
Our biggest uncertainty is the population diversity of the small sample, as well as the ecological validity of the data as all apnoeas were simulated and not true physiological events. 

### Biomedical Problem 
Our project aims to solve the issue of sleep disruption caused by respiratory complications, specifically sleep aponea. If successful in this project our software would provide early identification of subtle respiratory events that may predict sleep aponea, allowing for early intervention, encouragement of preventative measures and optimization of care. 

### Candidate Project 2
### Research Question 
We want to investigate whether an AI model trained on overnight sleep recordings from health adults in the [Sleep-EDF Database Expanded](https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/) can identify disordered sleep in patients with sleep disorders, including REM sleep behaviour disorder, in the [CAP Sleep Database](https://physionet.org/content/capslpdb/1.0.0/), and which features aid that identification. 

### Biggest Uncertainty 
Our biggest uncertainty is whether differences between the two datasets (recording setups and conditions, participant age, sleep-scoring conventions) will make sleep look abnormal for reasons unrelated to disease. 

### Biomedical Problem 
Our project aims to address the under-recognition of sleep disorders and provide a faster way to flag atypical sleep for a clinicians review, helping to prioritize patients for further assessment. For REM sleep behaviour disorder specifically, which can be an early warning sign of neurodegenerative disease, this could guide preventative measures and or prompt further assessment.
